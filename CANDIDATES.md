# Sick day excuse candidates (draft pass)

One line each, under 200 characters: an excuse that gets you
out of work AND gets a laugh.

1. Salary awareness: "Can't come in today -- I've come down
   with a sudden case of knowing exactly what my salary
   is." -- acute, untreatable by Friday.
2. The meeting: "I'm unwell: I attended yesterday's
   two-hour meeting in my dream and did the action items.
   I need the day to recover from work I imagined." --
   dream labour; the winner's structure, because the action
   items make it a completed shift.
3. Doctor's note: "My doctor says I have a severe case of
   Monday in my Tuesday. Bed rest and no standups
   prescribed." -- calendar illness.
4. Contagious: "Staying home: I've caught the deadline from
   the project and I'm highly contagious with follow-up
   questions." -- protective absence.
5. Honesty: "Sick today. Symptoms: nausea at the roadmap,
   dizziness at the org chart, full recovery expected by
   the weekend." -- the diagnostic list is the joke.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. A sick-day excuse must do two jobs --
absence and laugh -- and candidate 2 files a complete
timesheet for the absence: a two-hour meeting, attended in
full, action items done, all inside a dream, with recovery
time now owed for labour that never occurred. Candidate 1
is the sharper single line; candidate 2 is the one a manager
reads twice and forwards.
